## Talk: Solving Restoration Problems with Custom Kafka Streams State Stores

<div className={'flex justify-center pt-5'}>
    <img src={almogTalk.src} alt={"Solving State Restoration Problems Talk"} width={640}/>
</div>

In this talk [Almog Gavra](https://www.linkedin.com/in/agavra/) and [Sophie Blee-Goldman](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ableegoldman/) describe all the traps, gotchas and challenges in replacing the built-in RocksDB and In Memory State Stores with an external, hosted database. They will cover topics like how to maintain exactly once semantics with a remote store, how to fence zombie writers, and other fun topics!

- **When:** 19 March 2024, 1630-1715 GMT
- **Where:** Breakout Room 4

## Talk: Sizing and Operating Kafka Streams in Production

[Rohan Desai](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rohanpd/) will cover all the important topics related to operationalizing your Kafka Streams applications, including: How to you size your resources to run your application? How much cpu/memory/disk will you need? What kind of SLOs should you be setting for a Kafka Streams application? What should you be measuring/monitoring to know if your application is healthy? And more.

- **When:** 20 March 2024, 1000-1045 GMT
- **Where:** Breakout Room 6
